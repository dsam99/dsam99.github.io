Research

BATS Lab | Machine Learning Research Assistant
March 2019 - present

I am working on deriving new theoretical bounds to explain phenomena in weak supervision and domain adaptation. I develop experiments to illustrate the role of the symmetric divergence between two domains as a condition for learning. I attend weekly group meetings and theory meetings to discuss various topics in weak supervision and theoretical machine learning.

Rubenstein Lab | Computational Chemistry Research Assistant
November 2017 - present

I work on developing theory for the Brown Molecular Informatics Project. My project looks to better understand the information capacity of molecules in chemical space.

I extract a dataset of molecules from the GDB-13 database and project the dataset into mass spectra space by using the RDKit Cheminformatics and Machine Learning package. I perform analysis on the spacing between the datasets mass spectroscopy data. I implemented a pipeline to use the SPINUS prediction algorithm to generate NMR spectra. I create vectors of molecules representing the concatenation of mass spectra and NMR spectra to analyze a larger portion of chemical space.

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory | Deep Learning Research Intern
June 2019 - August 2019

This past summer, I was a deep learning research intern for the Mission Control Systems Deep Learning Group (393K). My project aimed to better understand and automate the Mars Rover Data transmission pipeline.

I built a data collector from 3 APIs from the data pipeline to construct a dataset of current and historical data transmissions from the Curiosity Rover. I implemented signal processing to compute relevant features and computed feature extraction to evaluate the importance of these features. The data collection software that I engineered is currently in production for the GDSA team for data visualization tools.

I implemented a deep neural network to identify failed data transmission with 95% accuracy, which is a 4% increase from the existing GDS software. I implemented an Adversarial Autoencoder and a One-Class SVM for anomaly detection in data transmission. My trained models are used by the GDSA team in real time analysis of data transmission and will likely be integrated for the Mars 2020 mission.

MIT Program for Information Science | Data Science Research Intern
May 2018 - September 2018

I was a Data Science Intern for the MIT Program of Information Science during the summer of 2018. I built the data pipeline to extract text data from multiple different sources, including news articles, the Twitter API, and the JSTOR database and create a training corpus. I implemented the data processing and text cleaning pipeline to convert text data into corresponding TF-IDF vectors. I performed statistical analysis on word frequencies and built n-grams models.

MIT Program for Information Science | Data Science Research Assistant
September 2018 - March 2019

Following my internship, I worked as a Data Science Research Assistant for the MIT Program on Information Science mentored by Micah Altman, the director of research.

I worked on the CensusUsage project, which is an open source research project aiming to categorize different use cases of the American Community Survey (ACS) data. Using the datasets that I had created, I analyzed the corpuses using unsupervised topic models based on LDA and NMF to identify articles and topics that fall within our pre-determined use cases. I implemented a LSTM as an alternative method to classify articles' use cases. The open source project can be found here.
