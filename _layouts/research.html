Research Experience

BATS Lab | Machine Learning Research Assistant
March 2019 - present

I am a research assistant in Stephen Bach's BATS Lab. I am working on deriving new theoretical bounds for the K-nearest neighbor algorithm under weakly supervised learning conditions. I am exploring different topics in probabilistic models, domain adaptation, and learning theory.

Rubenstein Lab | Computational Chemistry Research Assistant
November 2017 - present

I work on the theory team for the DARPA Molecular Informatics Project in Brenda Rubenstein's Lab. My project looks to better understand the information capacity of molecules of chemical space.
I data mined and web scraped mass spectroscopy data from chemical databases and visualized the characteristics of the mass space of the GDB13 dataset. I presented my results at the Brown Undergraduate Research Fair on April 3, 2018.
I am currently working on understanding NMR spectroscopic space; I have implemented a pipeline to use the SPINUS prediction algorithm to generate NMR vectors. Our lab is collaborating with the Fourches' Lab to perform different clustering algorithms on our dataset. We are currently working towards a journal submission.

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory | Deep Learning Research Intern
June 2019 - August 2019

This past summer, I was a deep learning research intern for the Mission Control Systems Deep Learning Group (393K). My project aimed to better understand and automate the Mars Rover Data transmission pipeline.
I built a data collector from 3 APIs from the data pipeline to construct a dataset of current and historical data transmissions from the Curiosity Rover. I implemented signal processing to compute relevant features and computed feature extraction to evaluate the importance of these features. The data collection software that I engineered is currently in production for the GDSA team for data visualization tools.
I implemented a deep neural network to identify failed data transmission with 95% accuracy, which is a 4% increase from the existing GDS software. I implemented an Adversarial Autoencoder and a One-Class SVM for anomaly detection in data transmission. My trained models are used by the GDSA team in real time analysis of data transmission and will likely be integrated for the Mars 2020 mission.

MIT Program for Information Science | Data Science Research Intern
May 2018 - March 2019

I was a data science research intern for the MIT Program of Information Science under Dr. Micah Altman. I worked on the CensusUsage project, which looked to categorize different use cases of the American Community Survey (ACS) data.
I created the data pipeline to mine from multiple different sources: web news articles, Twitter API, and the JSTOR database. I analyzed these datsets by using unsupervised topic models (LDA, NMF) to see if there was any relation between topics and use cases. I visualized different probability metrics and statistics about the dataset. The open data source is located here.
