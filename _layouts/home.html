About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a senior concentrating in Math and Computer Science at Brown University. I am from Tacoma, Washington.

My research interests span machine learning, robustness, and learning with limited labeled data. In the past, I've worked on developing performance-guaranteed methods to learn from dependent weak supervision sources and applications of machine learning to computational chemistry. I've also served as a research intern at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory working on using deep learning to automate Mars rover data transmission.

Outside of my studies and research, I play for the Brown Club Tennis Team and am an avid soccer player/fan. If you have any questions or are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch!
