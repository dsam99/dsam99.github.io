About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a second year PhD student in the Machine Learning Department at CMU, where I am fortunate to be advised by Zico Kolter. My work is supported by a NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and an ARCS Scholarship. My research interests are in developing machine learning and deep learning methods in settings with limited labeled data. This broadly includes semi-supervised learning, adapting pre-trained models, weak supervision/incorporating domain knowledge, and representation learning.

Before, I studied math and computer science at Brown University, where I was advised by Stephen Bach. In the past, I served as a research intern at Amazon AWS, MIT, and NASA JPL.

Outside of my studies and research, I enjoy playing tennis and am an avid soccer player/fan. If you are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch. I am always looking to form new collaborations!
