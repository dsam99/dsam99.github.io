About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a third year PhD student in the Machine Learning Department at CMU, where I am fortunate to be advised by Zico Kolter. My work is supported by a NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and an ARCS Scholarship. My research interests are in developing machine learning methods in settings with limited supervision. This broadly includes adapting and aligning large pretrained models, incorporating weak supervision or more general forms of domain knowledge, and generalization.

Before, I studied math and computer science at Brown University, where I was advised by Stephen Bach. In the past, I served as a research intern at Amazon AWS, the Bosch Center for AI, MIT, and NASA JPL. Outside of my studies and research, I enjoy playing tennis and am an avid soccer player/fan. If you are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch. I am always looking to chat about research and form new collaborations!
