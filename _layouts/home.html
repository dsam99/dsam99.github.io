About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a junior studying Math and Computer Science at Brown University. I am from Tacoma, Washington.

My interests lie in machine learning, deep learning, and learning theory. I currently am a research assistant in Stephen Bach's BATS Lab, focusing on theoretical machine learning and weak supervision. I am also a research assistant for Brenda Rubenstein's Lab, working on the Brown Molecular Informatics Project. Outside of my studies and research, I play for the Brown Club Tennis Team and am a Meiklejohn peer advisor. If you have any questions or are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch!
