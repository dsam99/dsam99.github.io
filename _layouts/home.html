About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm an incoming PhD student in the Machine Learning Department at CMU. I recently graduated from Brown University. I am originally from Tacoma, Washington.

My research interests span machine learning, robustness, and learning with limited labeled data. In the past, I've worked on developing performance-guaranteed methods to learn from weak supervision sources and applications of machine learning to computational chemistry. I've also served as a research intern at NASA JPL working on using deep learning methods to automate Mars rover data transmission and as a data science intern at MIT working on understanding use cases of census data.

Outside of my studies and research, I enjoy playing tennis and am an avid soccer player/fan. If you have any questions or are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch!
