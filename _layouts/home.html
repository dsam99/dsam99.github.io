About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a first year PhD student in the Machine Learning Department at CMU. I graduated from Brown University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Stephen Bach. I was also very fortunate to collaborate with Eli Upfal and Brenda Rubenstein. I am originally from Tacoma, Washington.

My research interests are in machine learning, especially in settings with limited labeled data. At Brown, I've worked on developing performance-guaranteed methods to learn from weak supervision sources. I've also served as a research intern at NASA JPL and a data science intern at MIT.

Outside of my studies and research, I enjoy playing tennis and am an avid soccer player/fan. If you have any questions or are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch!
