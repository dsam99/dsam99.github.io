About

Hi, I'm Dylan! I'm a senior studying Math and Computer Science at Brown University. I am from Tacoma, Washington.

My research interests span statistical machine learning, weakly supervised learning, and semi-supervised learning. I am currently working on an honors thesis providing theory for self-supervised learning under the mentorship of Professor Stephen Bach. Some of my past projects include developing theoretical guaranteed methods to learn from dependent weak supervision sources and the application of machine learning to computational chemistry.

Outside of my studies and research, I play for the Brown Club Tennis Team and am a Meiklejohn peer advisor. If you have any questions or are interested in my work, feel free to get in touch!
